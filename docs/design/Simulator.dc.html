Where should the cut-off sit?

A model that ranks customers well is useless until someone decides where to draw the line. Set what an offer costs and how often it works; the curve and the recommended threshold follow. Validation partition: 1,200 customers, 320 churners.

Simulated, not measured. The dataset holds no retention-campaign outcomes, so the offer success rate is an assumption you supply, not an estimate. The model estimates risk, not what an offer would change.

Why not simply take the peak?

The peak sits at {{peakLabel}} ({{peakNet}}). The recommended cut, {{recLabel}}, is {{gap}} lower: less than one offer ({{costLabel}}), on a figure built from an assumed success rate.

Cuts within one offer's cost of the maximum are treated as indistinguishable, and the tie breaks toward the highest threshold: the one that spends least and disturbs the fewest customers.
